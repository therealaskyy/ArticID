2. What data we process and why

  • Account data: player name, email, password (stored only as a non-reversible hash), Artic ID code, date and version of the Terms you accepted, creation date. Purpose: creating and managing your account and providing the service. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Date of birth: used to check the minimum age requirement (14 years). Legal basis: legal obligation and legitimate interest in protecting minors (Art. 6(1)(c) and 6(1)(f)).
  • Session data: a random sign-in identifier, stored as a hash, which keeps you signed in for 30 days. Legal basis: performance of a contract.
  • Security data: an attempt counter linked to a hash of your IP address or email, kept for a short time (15 minutes to 1 hour) to prevent abuse and password-guessing attacks. Your IP address is never stored in clear. Legal basis: legitimate interest in security (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Game saves: the progress data that games send to the cloud (levels, settings and similar), linked to your Artic ID. Legal basis: performance of a contract.
  • Service emails: if you ask for a password reset, we use your email to send you the link. We do not send marketing through Artic ID.

Providing the account data is necessary to create the Artic ID; without it we cannot provide the service.

3. Cookies

We use a single technical cookie, aid_session (HttpOnly, Secure), needed to keep you signed in. We use no profiling cookies and no third-party analytics on this page, so no consent banner is needed. Games may instead keep your sign-in token in the browser’s local storage on their own pages.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. It is processed on our behalf by the following processors: Vercel Inc. (hosting), Upstash, Inc. (database) and, for reset emails, Resend. Servers are set up in the European Union where possible. If a provider processes data outside the EEA, the transfer relies on appropriate safeguards (an adequacy decision such as the Data Privacy Framework, or standard contractual clauses). Artic Studio games you use with your Artic ID receive your player name and Artic ID code, and store your game saves in the cloud.

5. How long we keep data

We keep account data for as long as the account exists. If you delete it, we immediately delete your account, email, player name, sessions and cloud saves. Security data deletes itself within minutes. We may keep for longer only what the law requires us to keep.

6. Your rights

You can ask us for access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability of your data, and object to processing based on legitimate interest (Arts. 15–22 GDPR). You can delete your account yourself from the Artic ID page. For any other request write to [PRIVACY EMAIL]; we reply within one month. If you believe the processing breaches the law, you may lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority (garanteprivacy.it) or with the authority of your country.

7. Minors

Artic ID is for people aged 14 or over; below this age the account must be created by a parent. If we find that an account was opened by a child under 14 without a parent’s consent, we delete it.

8. Security

Passwords are stored with a strong hashing algorithm (scrypt) and a random salt; connections use HTTPS; session tokens are stored only as hashes; we limit sign-in attempts. No system is 100% secure: choose a long, unique password.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, let you know.
